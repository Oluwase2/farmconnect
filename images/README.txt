FarmConnect shows built-in pictures of each produce automatically. To use YOUR OWN photos instead,
put them in this folder named after the produce:

  maize.jpg  cassava.jpg  yam.jpg  tomatoes.jpg  pepper.jpg  rice.jpg  beans.jpg  onions.jpg
  hero.jpg   (optional wide banner photo behind the FarmConnect title)

- .jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work.
- For other produce use the lowercase name with no spaces, e.g. sweetpotato.jpg.
- Keep each file under about 500 KB.
- Use photos you are allowed to use (your own, or free-licence sites such as Unsplash or Pexels).
Refresh the page after adding photos.
